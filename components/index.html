Model version, interpretation & privacy

The scalar values sent for calculation are processed on the Streamlit server. ZIPs and report images stay in the browser extractor. Do not enter identifiers or upload PHI to an unapproved deployment. Browser processing alone is not a privacy/security approval.

Extracted values still require source verification. OCR accuracy is not guaranteed. The experimental observed-evidence assessment uses published precision references and observed trends and does not change the NKPI score.

Dataset & limitations

The score trajectory was fitted on 232 eligible adult eye pairs from 130 patients. The historical 426-eye inventory includes missing or ineligible follow-up records. The trajectory uses baseline NKPI and age; later measurements supply its training target.

Trajectory tuning and evaluation kept both eyes of a patient together. The NKPI formula itself was previously developed using overlapping records and remains frozen. This is conditional internal assessment, not independent external validation. The near-year check contains 45 eyes / 25 patients with visits at 9–15 months.

The selected CXL/control cohort and incomplete treatment/quality audit limit interpretation. There are two selected visits per eye, so the intermediate quarterly points are calculated, not measured. Displaying NKPI points does not improve the model’s accuracy or establish future progression or treatment benefit.
